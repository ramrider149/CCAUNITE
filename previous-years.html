How It Started

Our Story

In 2017, juniors Aadil Rehan and Melody Li and sophomore Catherine Zhang founded UNITE. Ever since, UNITE has been an annual event serving students, parents, and community members. We seek to spread the spirit of innovation and discovery through the unification of the many different individuals attending, and are proud to feature an opportunity for students and speakers alike to inspire and be inspired.

In 2025 — after a 3-year hiatus — seniors Aadi Jariwala, Kaelyn Liu, and Lucas Han assembled a team to bring UNITE back to CCA. This time with the theme of "Flourish": challenging what it means to thrive by looking beyond traditional success. The team redesigned CCA UNITE as a TEDx-style conference featuring student, alumni, and industry speakers, coupled with a robust club-day event, student performances, and non-traditional career interest exploration.

2025 Speakers

Flourish

We are proud to feature inspirational speakers that have truly made an impact in the community around them.

Shahriar Afshar

Founder and CEO of the Autism Innovation Lab, leading national efforts to advance inclusive housing, business, and innovation for the autism and IDD community. A retired public servant with over 30 years in public-private partnerships, he previously served as the Port of San Diego's Director of Government & Civic Relations and project lead for the $500 million San Diego Convention Center Expansion Project, and spent five years with Verizon managing municipal engagement initiatives across Southern California. He has founded and led multiple nonprofits, from local food pantries to national trade associations in Washington DC, and is a frequent media contributor and requested speaker on public policy, innovation, and inclusion.

Jeff Szekeres

Co-Founder and CEO of Compete for Causes LLC, driving strategy and executing philanthropic fitness events. He works full-time in Corporate Development at Danaher Corporation (NYSE: DHR). Jeff received an MBA in Finance with Honors from the Wharton School, a Master of Biotechnology from the University of Pennsylvania, and a bachelor's degree in human biology and genetics from Stanford University. He lives in Solana Beach, CA with his wife and three children.

Linda Litteral

A multi-faceted artist working across multiple media, with an MFA from San Diego State University. Her thesis explored art as a way to expose and heal childhood abuse. She has taught at SDSU, Mesa, Miramar, Grossmont, and Southwestern Colleges, as well as art-healing classes at Las Colinas, East Mesa, and Donovan State detention centers. She is a member of Allied Craftsman and emeritus director of the Feminist Image Group, and author of Show and Tell: Healing from Trauma through Art.

Michael Cherman

Designer, entrepreneur, and creative director best known as the founder of Market Studios (formerly Chinatown Market), a Los Angeles-based design studio recognized for collaborations with Converse, Crocs, Smiley, and the Grateful Dead. Known for blending streetwear, sport, and culture, Cherman combines design and storytelling to build products and experiences with a modern take on nostalgia.

Gabriella Bigler

A 16-year-old junior at CCA who has lived in San Diego for five years and is passionate about community involvement. An athlete, student, and artist who is also legally blind due to various eye conditions since birth, she has learned to embrace her impairment and flourish in new ways through it.

Ethan Leem

A sixteen-year-old junior at Canyon Crest Academy passionate about indigenous studies, who began working with the Maasai tribe in Kenya this year to learn about and bring their philosophies to the Western world. Ethan believes indigenous knowledge can create scalable solutions to the world's modern problems.

Daniel Zhang

Co-Founder and President of STEAMLabs International, having led 30+ free science nights and camps, grown 11 chapters across the U.S. and abroad, and built a team of 100+ volunteers reaching thousands of underserved students. A High School Affiliate Scholar at the UC Davis Comprehensive Cancer Center, he also directs a music outreach program for seniors and has performed at Carnegie Hall and Tokyo Opera Hall as an all-state pianist.

Avani Goyal

A sophomore at CCA who thrives on exploring the intersections of creativity, leadership, and innovation, from building robots to planning events. Her talk explores the beauty of individuality and why success isn't a "one size fits all," inspiring others to flourish on their own terms.

2018–2019 Speakers

Featured voices from UNITE's second year

Richard Kuo

Richard Kuo is a youth inspirational speaker, mentor, and Youtuber with over 1 million video views. He has been featured at TEDx and has also been invited to speak at over 100 schools across the United States to help students realize their significance. Growing up with very low self-esteem, a negative self-image, depression, and other teenage problems, Richard understands the challenges many students face today. He uses his own life story of overcoming negative self-talk to empower students to take ownership of their lives today.

Viet Mai

An educator, poet, and entrepreneur from San Diego who found his voice through spoken word poetry. He connects with community leaders throughout San Diego to inspire young people, promote civic engagement, and elevate social consciousness, and currently serves with the Village of Promise Collective Mentoring Program.

Jennifer Purdie

Author of the bestselling Growth Mindset for Athletes, Coaches and Trainers, a freelance writer, endurance athlete, TEDx speaker, and certified personal trainer. An Ironman finisher and one of the first Americans to complete marathons on every continent, she has written for the LA Times, Washington Post, and more.

Chris Hamilton

Began teaching 18th and 19th Century British Literature at UCLA before moving into SAT/ACT instruction in 1998. Over the last decade he has founded or co-founded three education companies devoted to skills-based test prep and college consulting, helping students earn admission to Harvard and other top schools.

Sofia Perri

A senior at Canyon Crest Academy involved in Associated Student Body, Varsity Soccer, and ADL's No Place for Hate Club, among many other activities. She continued her education at the University of Michigan in Ann Arbor, studying Sport Management.

Alexander Simone

A San Francisco-based entrepreneur, inventor, and designer who grew up in San Diego and attended Canyon Crest Academy. Alexander founded Pronto Concepts Inc in 2016 and appeared on Season 9 of Shark Tank pitching ProntoBev, a 30-second wine chiller.

2017–2018 Speakers

The very first UNITE lineup

Sonja Bruzauskas

Born and raised in Germany, mezzo soprano Sonja Bruzauskas was trained on both sides of the Atlantic Ocean. Besides her expansive stage career in musical theater, opera and operetta, Sonja is a well-established concert singer and recitalist, focusing on oratorio, Art Song and contemporary music. Sonja has performed and lectured at institutions such as Vanderbilt, LSU, Rice University, Cal Arts, the University of Houston, and the Philosophical Society at Rice University. Sonja is the founder and director of pARTnerschools, a project that connects students through the Arts to empower diversity and strengthen communities.

Lex Gillette

Lex Gillette is a globally recognized Paralympic track and field athlete who competes for Team USA. Competing primarily in the long jump, Lex has acquired four Paralympic medals, three world champion titles, and 18 national championships. He is the current world record holder in the long jump for totally blind athletes with a leap of 22 ft 1 in, making him the only totally blind athlete on the planet to ever eclipse the 22 ft barrier in the event. When he is not competing, Lex is a motivational speaker who has presented to Fortune 500 corporations, pro sports teams, trade associations, and universities.

Annie Reilly

A senior at Canyon Crest Academy who completed her yoga teacher certification (RYT 200) over the summer of 2017 and volunteers on campus teaching yoga to students and staff, aiming to bring awareness and presence to her students in an energizing, fun, and relaxing way.

Theo Zelkind

A 17-year-old junior at CCA, born and raised in San Diego, passionate about making positive change in the world. Theo has spoken about his experience as a member of the LGBT+ and Jewish communities, sharing a message of love and unity with CCA UNITE.

Kacee Jensen

Founder of Let's Talk Teens, a resource hub helping parents and teens navigate the modern world. A youth advocate, speaker, writer, and mom of four, Kacee has spent 16 years helping families, schools, and communities across the country, and speaks nationally on behalf of Rachel's Challenge.
