The Theme

What is Radiance?

Every idea starts as a single spark: one person's curiosity, obsession, or quiet conviction. On its own, it's small. It only becomes radiance when others reflect it back, when a listener carries it further, adds their own light to it, and passes it on again.

That's the shape of this event. A speaker lights the first spark. The room reflects it. And by the time it leaves the building, it's grown into something none of us could have made alone.
