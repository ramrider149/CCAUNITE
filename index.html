The Theme

What is Radiance?

Every idea starts as a single spark: one person's curiosity, obsession, or quiet conviction. On its own, it's small. It only becomes radiance when others reflect it back, when a listener carries it further, adds their own light to it, and passes it on again.

That's the shape of this event. A speaker lights the first spark. The room reflects it. And by the time it leaves the building, it's grown into something none of us could have made alone.

Meet This Year's Speakers

Meet the voices bringing Radiance to the stage this year.

Smera Karna

9th Grade
About the Speech

Her speech will explore the fear of being bad at something and how that fear can prevent people from trying new things. Through her experience starting Bicode, she will show that growth begins when people are willing to be beginners.

Her audience will take away the idea that radiance is not about being the best or already knowing how to succeed, but having the courage to try before knowing whether you will shine.

Extracurriculars & Hobbies

In her free time, she enjoys robotics and engineering, where she is part of an FTC team. She also enjoys tutoring students through Bicode, helping them build their understanding and confidence in subjects such as math, science, and computer science.
